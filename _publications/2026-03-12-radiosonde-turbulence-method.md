This study introduces a method for estimating atmospheric turbulence from high-resolution radiosonde observations using the minimum Richardson number. The method accounts for wind shear and static stability, enabling turbulence detection in both overturning and statically stable layers.

We evaluate the estimates against commercial aircraft observations and apply the method to ten years of global radiosonde data to examine the geographical and seasonal distributions of atmospheric turbulence.
