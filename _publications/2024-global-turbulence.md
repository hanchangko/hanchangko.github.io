We use six years of operational high-resolution radiosonde observations to map atmospheric turbulence across much of the globe with the Thorpe method.

The analysis reveals distinct regional, vertical, and seasonal patterns: turbulence is generally stronger in the troposphere, with a summer maximum, while stratospheric turbulence peaks in spring. Radiosondes also provide information over regions and at altitudes poorly sampled by commercial aircraft, extending the observational basis for studying turbulence and its aviation implications.
