We evaluate ECMWF's operational aviation turbulence forecasts against a year of commercial aircraft measurements. Although the forecasts distinguish turbulent from less turbulent conditions reasonably well, they substantially underestimate the occurrence of moderate-or-greater turbulence.

We test two recalibration approaches using observed turbulence statistics and optimized distribution parameters. Both improve forecast performance, with additional evaluation using independent research aircraft observations. The study demonstrates how aircraft measurements can guide practical improvements to turbulence forecasting.
