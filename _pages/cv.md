## Research Interests

- Observational characteristics of turbulence in the free atmosphere
- Sources, generation mechanisms, and climatological variability of atmospheric turbulence
- Development and validation of observation-based turbulence estimation methods
- Aviation turbulence forecasting and model evaluation using aircraft and radiosonde observations
- Impacts of climate change and solar geoengineering on atmospheric turbulence and aviation hazards
